Privacy Policy for AdBlocker

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AdBlocker Chrome extension ("the extension", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

Summary

AdBlocker does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data or browsing activity. All ad-blocking happens locally in your browser.

What the extension does

AdBlocker blocks network requests to known advertising and tracking domains, and hides leftover ad elements on web pages you visit. This processing happens entirely on your device.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your browsing history
  • The content of pages you visit
  • Personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Any analytics or usage statistics about how you use the extension

Local storage

The extension uses your browser's local storage only to remember whether you have turned ad-blocking on or off. This setting never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Permissions

The extension requests browser permissions (such as declarativeNetRequest and host permissions) solely to block ad/tracking requests and hide ad elements on websites. These permissions are not used to collect or transmit data about you.

Third parties

AdBlocker does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the extension's GitHub repository.
